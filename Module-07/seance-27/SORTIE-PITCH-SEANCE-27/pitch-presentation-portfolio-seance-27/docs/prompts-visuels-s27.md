# Prompts des visuels S27

**Date de génération :** 2026-09-04  
**Mode :** outil intégré de génération d’images  
**Autorisation humaine :** création et intégration de visuels autorisées pour la PHASE 6  
**Statut :** deux visuels générés et intégrés — validation humaine requise

## 1. Visuel principal

**Fichier :** `public/assets/images/hero-workflow-s27-v1.png`  
**Dimensions :** 1 672 × 941 px  
**Usage :** illustration principale du premier écran

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: website hero image and social preview for a professional portfolio pitch
Primary request: Create an original premium editorial illustration representing a human-controlled AI workflow for engineering project monitoring. Show an abstract luminous project dashboard made of clean data panels and seven small alert markers, connected to a deliberate upward path with three visual stages: observation, human decision, verified result. Include a subtle human hand interacting with one control point, emphasizing human approval.
Scene/backdrop: deep navy technical environment with faint architectural grid lines and restrained engineering schematics; no identifiable building or real project.
Style/medium: sophisticated vector-like 3D editorial illustration, polished but credible, not photorealistic, not futuristic fantasy.
Composition/framing: wide landscape composition; visual focus on the right half; generous dark negative space on the left for website headline; strong depth and clear silhouette.
Lighting/mood: controlled cyan glow with limited warm gold accents; confident, precise, calm.
Color palette: deep navy, off-white highlights, turquoise/cyan, restrained gold.
Constraints: no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no trademarks, no real company data, no client names, no maps, no watermark, no UI labels, no excessive neon, no clutter. The image must remain readable when cropped responsively.
```

## 2. Carte de présentation

**Fichier :** `public/og.png`  
**Dimensions :** 1 568 × 1 003 px  
**Usage :** carte de présentation dans la section Ressources ; actif préparé pour une future métadonnée sociale après confirmation d’une origine publique

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: Open Graph social preview card for a professional portfolio presentation website
Primary request: Create a clean, premium landscape social card for the engineering project presentation INGÉNIA PILOT. Use a deep navy technical background with subtle blueprint lines, a restrained turquoise data-flow motif, and one small gold verification accent. The composition should feel professional, credible, precise, and human-controlled.
Style/medium: polished editorial graphic, vector-like, minimal, high contrast.
Composition/framing: wide landscape card; centered typographic hierarchy; generous safe margins; no tiny text.
Color palette: deep navy, off-white, turquoise, restrained gold.
Text (verbatim): "INGÉNIA PILOT" and below it "L’IA ASSISTE. L’HUMAIN DÉCIDE."
Constraints: render exactly those two lines and no other text; accents and apostrophes must be exact; no numbers; no company logos; no trademarks; no real project data; no watermark; no decorative text; no certificate or award imagery.
Avoid: misspelled French, extra letters, excessive neon, clutter, stock-photo style, realistic buildings, identifiable people.
```

## 3. Contrôles

- absence de donnée professionnelle réelle ;
- absence de logo ou marque tierce ;
- absence de watermark ;
- cohérence avec la palette bleu nuit, turquoise et or ;
- texte de la carte relu visuellement ;
- originaux de génération conservés hors du projet ;
- copies finales enregistrées dans le projet.

La métadonnée `og:image` n’est pas activée pendant la PHASE 6, car aucune URL
publique fiable du futur site S27 n’est encore disponible. L’actif pourra être
relié avec une URL absolue après autorisation de publication.
